## Quota pace and context guidance

Quota pace compares used percentage with the elapsed share of the reported window, aligned to the reading's timestamp. A five-percentage-point tolerance defines **On track**; lower readings show **Below pace**, higher readings **Ahead of pace**, and 100% **Limit reached**. Guidance includes a marker on the usage bar, projected use at reset after 5% of the window has elapsed, remaining hourly/daily allowance, and advice to ease usage when ahead. Stale, failed, missing, expired, and inconsistent readings withhold the pace classification. The guide explicitly assumes evenly spread use over the full window.

Context guidance uses actual measured room and up to five consecutive growth increments, requiring three compatible readings. It stops at missing data, changed settings/model/window, or a decrease. Room/watch/near-full/full thresholds use measured percentages; fast growth warns when about three or fewer similar exchanges would reach 80%. Draft estimates have separate warnings and never move measured context or its status. These calculations use existing readings and saved replies without extra provider queries.
